Company Overview

Koketjo Kamogelo Trading Enterprise (Pty) Ltd was founded and established in 2016 in Mokopane. Our company is dedicated to the principle of Botho Arethusaneng (working together in harmony).

What makes us stand out is our unique combination of management expertise, sales and distribution, high-quality innovative products, and exceptional customer service. We were formed to foster SMME (Small, Medium and Micro Enterprises) development objectives.

As a core business, we specialize in towing, mechanical services, and general services. Our team consists of well-trained and experienced personnel who provide quality goods and services 24 hours a day. Our customers are extremely important to us and always come first.

"We promise to provide our clients exceptional services with a personal touch."

Our Mission

Our primary aim is to open more job opportunities for skilled individuals who have been denied a chance to prove themselves. We create job opportunities for local communities where our business operates and empower them through social responsibility programs.

  • Create employment for skilled individuals
  • Support local community development
  • Implement social responsibility programs

Our Vision

To be a globally recognized service provider and partner to chosen markets, achieving outstanding results for our clients and partners through quality management programs that steer our team of experts to provide service satisfaction.

Our Policies & Commitments

Health & Safety Policy

Koketjo Kamogelo Trading Enterprise implements procedures to prevent injury or loss and assure the health and safety of our employees.

We emphasize conservation of our environment and the well-being of our communities and employees.
